- Face-to-face conversation: 音声翻訳 can listen to the microphone (audio source「マイク」, or「対面会話 開始」in the command palette). Two people share one microphone and one screen, each speaking their own language; every utterance is recognized in whichever of the two languages it is in and translated into the other one. Your own lines appear on the left, the other person's on the right. Your language is the target language of the settings; the other person's is chosen in the window's title bar. Room noise between sentences is not sent to recognition: the threshold follows the noise level of the microphone.
