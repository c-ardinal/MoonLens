- Live subtitles: once a whole sentence has been recognized it is translated again in the background, as one sentence and with the earlier sentences as context, and the result replaces the fragment-by-fragment translation in the window. Two settings under 音声翻訳 control the load: リアルタイム補正 (correct each sentence as soon as it ends) and 一括補正 (correct what is left when the speech pauses or the audio ends). Both are on by default.
