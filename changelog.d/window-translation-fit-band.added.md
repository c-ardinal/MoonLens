- Window translation: a window can fit itself to the subtitle band. "字幕領域へ整形" in the title bar observes where text appears for 15–45 seconds, leaves fixed text (HUD, logos) out, and shrinks the window to the band where lines keep changing, so less of the screen is read on every change. The window then stays as fitted; the same button puts it back to its former place and size. Works from a maximized window too.
