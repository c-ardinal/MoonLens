- Window translation: a composite window for streaming and recording (Windows). Switch "配信用ウィンドウ" on in the attach menu of a window attached to an app, and MoonLens opens a window that shows that app's window with the translations drawn over it. Pick it in the window capture of OBS, Discord and similar tools to stream the app with its translations. It follows the app window's size, includes every translation window attached to that app, and closes with the last attachment or the app.
