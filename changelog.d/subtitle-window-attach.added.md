- Audio translation windows can be attached to a window of another app (new "attach" header button). Choose where the subtitles sit: inside the app window along its bottom edge, outside above, below, left or right of it, or freely placed. The window follows the app window as it moves and resizes, hides while it is minimized, and gets its own size back when detached or when the app window closes. On Windows, attaching also switches the window to that app's audio, and detaching restores the previous audio source.
