- Per-app glossaries: under Settings → 翻訳 → 用語集, add terms for a single app (by the name of its executable; apps that have a window open are offered). They are used on top of the common glossary, and win over it, whenever the text comes from that app: a translation window attached to one of its windows, or 音声翻訳 listening to that app's audio. Nothing has to be switched by hand, and the cached translations of other apps stay as they are.
