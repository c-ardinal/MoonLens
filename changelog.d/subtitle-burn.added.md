- File translation: after subtitles are made for a video, export a new copy of it with the subtitles (translation, original or both) drawn at the bottom. Needs ffmpeg with libass, which you get and place yourself (next to MoonLens, on the PATH, or chosen under Settings → 音声翻訳 → ffmpeg の場所); MoonLens neither ships nor downloads it. Uses a GPU encoder when one works, and memory stays flat whatever the length.
