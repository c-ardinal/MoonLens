- Models that went unused are freed automatically: the translation model, the general model (explain / summarize) and the text recogniser release their memory and VRAM after 10 minutes without use (`performance.model_idle_unload_minutes`, 0 = keep loaded; Settings → 未使用モデルの自動解放). Open translation windows and running subtitles keep their models loaded, and opening a window loads them ahead of the first reading.
