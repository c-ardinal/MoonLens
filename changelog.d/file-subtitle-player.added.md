- ファイル翻訳 plays the video (or audio) whose subtitles it generates, with the subtitles shown in sync on the picture: translation, original or both (the same choice as for saving). Playback can start while subtitles are still being generated; it pauses just before the point the generation has reached and resumes once enough is ready. The subtitle list highlights the line being played and jumps there on click. Formats the built-in player cannot play show a note instead; generation and saving work as before. Everything stays on the device.
